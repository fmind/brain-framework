# Prepare knowledge for another brain

Use when asked to prepare or transfer selected knowledge to a named audience. Federation grants retrieval context; it does not grant publication permission. Preparing a candidate is separate from writing into the destination or contacting its readers, unless the task already authorizes those actions.

1. Select the specific concepts or decisions and the destination audience. Read the destination's instructions and schema within authorized access. Start with at most five notes; do not recursively export the graph or copy private action inputs.
1. Write a candidate under the current action's `outputs/`, preserving uncertainty and the owner's stance. Include only evidence the destination audience can access and may retain. Review text, frontmatter, URLs, titles, identities, attachments and links; removing a private paragraph while leaving its identifiers or backlinks does not remove the disclosure.
1. Keep a private source-to-destination mapping and source revision captures with the action's private inputs. The shareable manifest lists only destination paths, included files and accessible citations. It must not reveal private source paths, hashes, names or why an undisclosed source was omitted. If accessible evidence is insufficient, label the conclusion as an attributed assessment or omit it.
1. Check each included link deliberately. Remap authored BF links to destination notes and declare compatible roles there; equal schema names do not guarantee equal meanings. Copy no `brains:` references to personal brains, sensors, routines or machine trust. Anything needed but omitted remains an explicit dependency gap. Review the prepared diff before an external write requiring approval.
1. Validate and evaluate the candidate in a disposable destination brain with **no source-brain references**. Test a question a teammate needs answered, missing evidence, and whether any private source identity or passage survived. `bf validate` cannot establish privacy and permits unresolved foreign links; inspect those separately before claiming the package is self-contained.
1. For an authorized transfer, preserve destination edits. On a later refresh, compare the prior shared version, the newly prepared version and current destination; show conflicts instead of replacing the destination wholesale. Update the private mapping and the destination retrieval case after validation. Commit or publish only within existing authorization.

This is a reviewed Markdown transfer, not an access-control or automatic redaction system. All readers of the destination brain can read its files, including Git history.
