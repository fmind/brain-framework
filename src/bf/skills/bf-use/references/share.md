# Prepare knowledge for another brain

Use when asked to prepare or transfer selected knowledge to a named audience. Federation grants retrieval context, not publication permission, and preparing a candidate never authorizes writing into the destination or contacting its readers.

1. Select the specific concepts or decisions and the audience, and read the destination's instructions and declared `fields:` within authorized access. Start with at most five notes; never export the graph recursively or copy private action inputs.
1. Write the candidate in the owning action's `outputs/`, or else in a task-owned private staging directory with separate candidate and private-evidence folders. Include only evidence the audience can access and may retain, keeping uncertainty and the owner's stance. Review text, frontmatter, URLs, titles, identities, attachments and links: removing a private paragraph while leaving its identifiers or backlinks does not remove the disclosure.
1. Keep the source-to-destination mapping and source captures with the private evidence, outside the candidate. The shareable manifest lists only destination paths, included files and accessible citations, never private source paths, hashes, names or why an undisclosed source was omitted. Without accessible evidence, label a conclusion as an attributed assessment or omit it. Before removing staging, move what a later refresh needs to an authorized private location, never the destination brain.
1. Remap authored BF links to destination notes and declare compatible relations there: equal relation names do not guarantee equal meanings. Rewrite or drop the source brain's `entity`, aliases and `resource`, and copy no `brains:` references, sensors, routines or machine registrations. Report anything needed but omitted as a dependency gap.
1. Validate and evaluate the candidate in a disposable destination brain with **no source-brain references**: a question a teammate needs answered, missing evidence and any surviving private identity or passage. `bf validate` cannot establish privacy and permits unresolved foreign links: inspect those yourself.
1. On a later refresh, compare the prior shared version, the new candidate and the current destination, and show conflicts instead of replacing destination edits. Commit or publish only within existing authorization.

This is a reviewed Markdown transfer, not access control or automatic redaction: every reader of the destination brain can read its files, including Git history.
