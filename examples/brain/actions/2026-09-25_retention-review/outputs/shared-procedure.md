# Selected evidence capture

Proposed procedure for review: before a consequential decision, retain a selected capture of the evidence used, with its original accessible citation, observation date and limitations. On review, compare that retained body with the current source. Treat a changed source as a reason to inspect the decision, not proof that it is wrong.

Use only sources this brain's readers may access and retain. Partial evidence remains partial after capture. A digest detects accidental alteration; it does not authenticate a claim. Keep captures with the authorized action's inputs and follow the destination's retention policy.

No successful outcome or verification is asserted. Before adoption, try a synthetic source replacement and confirm that the original body is still available. This is a draft proposal with no required source-brain connection.
