# Selected evidence capture

Proposed procedure: before a consequential decision, save the exact evidence revision with the action; cite its original ref and the local capture; compare it with a fresh exact read when reviewing the decision. Apply when the upstream source can overwrite a body. Do not copy unrelated material or assume a digest proves truth.

The fictional retention decision motivates this draft. Counterexample: a partial source cannot establish a complete account even when its capture is intact. Missing check: try the procedure on a separate synthetic case that was not used to derive it. No verification or successful reuse is asserted.
